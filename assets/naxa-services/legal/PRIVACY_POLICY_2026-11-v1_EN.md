# NAXA SERVICES — Privacy Policy
**Draft version: 2026-11-v1 · English · Subject to legal and compliance review before commercial use**

> This is a drafting document. Add the publisher's legal identity, privacy contact, actual service providers, retention schedule and applicable legal bases before publication.

## 1. Controller and contact
The publisher's legal name, address, representative and privacy contact must be provided before release. Each business owner is responsible for the legality and accuracy of personal information entered into its organization space.

## 2. Data categories
Depending on the business profile, enabled features and organization choices, NAXA SERVICES may process:
- staff identity, internal login ID, role, organization, sites, workstations and session history;
- customer and supplier contact details, preferences, service history, orders, appointments, tickets and complaints;
- sales, payment, receipt, invoice, credit, refund, expense, objective, inventory, purchasing, commission and reporting data;
- attendance and working-hour data when those features are enabled;
- technical security, device, access, administrative action and synchronization logs;
- attachments, photos, documents and receipts intentionally added by users;
- language, theme, notification, backup and document-acceptance preferences.

Optional or sensitive fields should be minimized. Medical, biometric or unrelated personal data must not be collected in ordinary business profiles.

## 3. Purposes
Data may be used to provide business management features, authenticate users, secure accounts, apply roles, manage subscriptions and payments, synchronize operations, create receipts and reports, provide support, send alerts and improve reliability. Each organization must remain isolated. Staff can view only records within their permissions.

## 4. Legal bases and consent
The legal basis for each processing purpose must be documented, such as contract performance, legal obligation, legitimate interests or consent as permitted by law. Where consent is required (photos, marketing contact, device permissions, calls/screen sharing, optional SMS-based payment verification or Google-account backup), it must be specific, informed, dated, auditable and revocable where required. Accepting Terms must not be treated as blanket consent for optional processing.

## 5. Payment and financial data
Payment secrets, PINs and OTPs must never be requested or stored. A transaction reference may be retained for reconciliation and audit. A notification or user declaration is not proof of payment. Sensitive details should be minimized on lock-screen notifications.

## 6. Artificial intelligence
AI may analyze business data the user is authorized to access to produce explanations, anomalies and estimates. Responses should identify their sources, periods and limitations. One SME's data must not feed another SME's answers. Phone numbers, raw SMS, PINs, OTPs and secrets must not be transmitted to external models. Any transfer to an AI provider and processing country must be documented and approved before activation.

## 7. Support, calls and screen sharing
Support requests contain information voluntarily provided by the user. Chat, calls and screen sharing should clearly identify the agent, purpose, duration and sharing indicator. Sharing must be stoppable immediately. Recording is off by default. Any retention must be justified, disclosed and legally permitted.

## 8. Hosting and service providers
Actual providers used for hosting, database, push, backup, AI, payments, file storage and support must be listed in an updated annex with their role, processing location, commitments, retention and transfer details. Providers that are not connected must not be presented as active.

## 9. Retention and deletion
Retention periods depend on purpose, accounting duties, disputes, security and contract. A schedule must specify periods by data category. Audit logs and financial documents may be retained under applicable obligations. Access, correction, objection, restriction, deletion and portability requests are handled under applicable law, subject to legal exceptions and mandatory retention.

## 10. Security
Intended safeguards include role and scope access control, organization/site isolation, encrypted communications, protected session tokens, audit logging, device management, encrypted backups, unauthorized-access testing, restricted administrator access and incident response. Privileged keys must never be embedded in Android applications.

## 11. Offline data and backups
Some data required for work may be stored locally on the device, including while offline. Local data may remain on the device until synchronized or exported under the published policy. Google Drive backups should be optional, encrypted, limited to the selected data scope and restorable only by an authorized user. Authentication sessions and tokens must not be copied into ordinary backup archives.

## 12. Individual rights and complaints
The customer organization must inform its customers, employees, contractors and suppliers about processing for which it is responsible and provide an appropriate contact. The publisher must define request intake, identity verification and response procedures. Official contact details and response deadlines must be completed.

## 13. Children and vulnerable persons
Children's data must not be collected in NAXA SERVICES unless necessary, lawful and covered by specific safeguards. Products and organizations processing children's data require a separate assessment.

## 14. Changes
Each new version is identified, dated and presented to affected users where required. Previous acceptance evidence must not be overwritten.
