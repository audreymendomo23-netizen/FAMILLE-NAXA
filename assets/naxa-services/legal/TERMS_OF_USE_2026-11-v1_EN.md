# NAXA SERVICES — Terms of Use
**Draft version: 2026-11-v1 · English · Subject to legal review before commercial use**

> This is a functional drafting document, not legal advice. The publisher's legal identity, address, support contacts, applicable law, commercial terms and service-provider annexes must be completed by the publisher and reviewed by qualified counsel in Cameroon before publication.

## 1. Purpose and acceptance
NAXA SERVICES is a management software tool for service businesses. Access is limited to people whose credentials were created by the business owner or an authorized manager. Public self-registration is not available. Users must review these Terms and the Privacy Policy and accept each document separately. The application records the document version, language, account and server timestamp when online.

## 2. Accounts and responsibilities
Each user must use their own login and protect their password. Sharing credentials is prohibited. The organization owner is responsible for creating accounts, assigning roles, revoking former staff and verifying permissions. Access may be suspended when compromised or when needed to protect the service.

## 3. Organization, activity and scopes
The organization owner selects the main business activity during first setup. The selection configures the ERP screens and workflows and is locked for the organization. An exceptional correction process may be provided if administered, audited and compatible with existing records. Activity profile is separate from organization structure:
- standalone SME: one location and its workstations;
- multiple workstations within one location: shared site cash and stock by default, with workstation analytics where configured;
- multi-site network: each site retains its own sales, cash sessions, inventory, staff and operations. Network views are consolidated according to access rights without merging financial entries.

Users may access only the sites, workstations and features granted to them.

## 4. Data accuracy and business operations
The customer is responsible for accurate data, prices, catalogues, inventory, schedules, invoices, expenses, cash operations, taxes and staff information. Approved operations must not be silently deleted. Corrections, cancellations, refunds and adjustments must be justified and logged. Calculations must be checked before being used as external accounting or legal evidence.

## 5. Payments and subscriptions
A "I have paid" declaration, manually entered reference or forwarded SMS alone is not proof of confirmed payment. Subscription activation depends on reliable reconciliation or authorized audited validation. The applicable plan, price, quota, duration and renewal terms are those presented at purchase. Paid features may be suspended at expiry without deleting business data. Rights are server-controlled and cannot be granted by local edits.

## 6. Offline use and synchronization
Available local features may continue without a network, subject to subscription and security rules. Pending events are kept until durable server acknowledgement or documented conflict resolution. Financial events must not be replayed as a new sale or payment after a timeout. The device clock is not a substitute for server time. Offline tolerance depends on the confirmed plan.

## 7. Receipts, invoices and documents
Receipts, invoices, quotes, credit notes and exports are produced from entered data and enabled workflows. The business must verify their compliance with tax, accounting and contractual requirements before providing them externally. Download, sharing and printing depend on device and customer peripherals.

## 8. Inventory, services and entrusted property
The business is responsible for the accuracy of stock, purchase cost, consumption, losses, counts, movements and transfers. For laundry services, the business must display and agree with its customers on drop-off, identification, pickup, unclaimed item retention, complaints and liability rules. NAXA SERVICES does not take possession of clothing, products, vehicles, devices or other property handled by the customer business.

## 9. Accounting, tax and AI
Reports and calculations are management tools and do not constitute certified accounting, tax, financial or legal advice. Applicable regimes, rates, filings and obligations must be validated by a qualified professional. AI suggestions are indicative, explainable and subject to human decision. AI does not confirm payments, approve refunds, sign filings or replace an accountant or tax professional.

## 10. Support and communications
Support may include FAQ, support requests and, after activation of the relevant services, chat, calls or screen sharing. Each media session requires explicit user action and consent. Screen sharing may expose visible information; users should close or hide unrelated data. Recording must not occur without notice and valid consent.

## 11. Availability, backups and security
Users must protect their devices and credentials. Backups should be encrypted, verifiable and restored only by authorized users. Backups do not replace legal and accounting record retention. Availability of network, storage, notifications and payment providers may depend on third parties.

## 12. Prohibited use
Prohibited conduct includes sharing secrets, unauthorized access to third-party data, falsifying documents, bypassing permissions, abusive extraction, payment fraud and unlawful use.

## 13. Suspension, termination and export
Subscription expiry or suspension may restrict operational rights. Data rules must preserve unsynchronized local events and provide, where legally and securely appropriate, export, contractual access or renewal functions. Removing a member's account must not delete organization-owned records.

## 14. Intellectual property and feedback
NAXA SERVICES brands, interfaces, software, graphics and components remain subject to their owners' rights. Customers retain rights in their business data and documents. Any license, reuse, portability or return obligations must be described in the final commercial terms.

## 15. Governing law and contact
Applicable law, competent courts, publisher identity, complaint deadlines, billing, termination and official support details must be completed and legally reviewed before publication.
